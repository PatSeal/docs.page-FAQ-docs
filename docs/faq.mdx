## Why is my dashboard empty?

You'll need your church administrator to approve your account before you can see anything beyond public events. See [Home & dashboard](/using-the-app/home-and-dashboard).

## I didn't get a confirmation email — what do I do?

Check your spam folder first. If it's still not there after a few minutes, contact your church administrator.

## Can I use Avodah on Android and iPhone?

Yes — Avodah works in any modern mobile browser and can be added to your home screen on both. See [Installing to your home screen](/getting-started/installing-to-your-home-screen).

## How do I get a receipt for a gift I made?

Open **Giving → History** and download it from there. See [Giving history & receipts](/giving/giving-history-and-receipts).

## Who do I contact if something's not working?

Reach out to your church administrator — they can look into account-specific issues or escalate a bug.
