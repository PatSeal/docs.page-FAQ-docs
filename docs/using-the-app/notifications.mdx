---
title: Turning on notifications
description: Enable push notifications so you don't miss updates.
---

1. Close your browser and open Avodah from your home screen icon.
2. Open the sidebar menu.
3. Select **Notifications**.
4. Toggle push notifications on, and allow the permission prompt when it appears.

You'll now get notified about the things your church administrator sends out — event reminders, announcements, and updates from your groups.
