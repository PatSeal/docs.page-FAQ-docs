---
title: Profile & account settings
description: Update your name, photo, and contact details.
---

Open the sidebar menu and select **Profile** to update your name, photo, phone number, and email address at any time.

## Changing your password

From your profile, select **Account Settings → Change Password**. You'll need your current password to set a new one.

## Deleting your account

Contact your church administrator to request account deletion — this isn't currently a self-service action.
