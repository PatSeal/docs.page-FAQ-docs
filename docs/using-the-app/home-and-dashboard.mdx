Your home dashboard is the first thing you see after logging in. From here you can jump to your groups, upcoming events, and giving — and see anything that needs your attention.

## If your dashboard looks empty

If you're only seeing public events and nothing else, your church administrator hasn't approved your account for full access yet. Reach out to them to be granted access to your church's internal features.
