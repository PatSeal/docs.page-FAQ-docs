1. Open Avodah from your home screen icon, or go back to the login page in your browser.
2. Enter the email address and password you signed up with.
3. Tap **Log In**.

## Forgot your password?

Tap **Forgot password** on the login screen and follow the reset link sent to your email. If you no longer have access to that email address, contact your church administrator to update it on your account.
