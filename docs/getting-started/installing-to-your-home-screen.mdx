---
title: Installing to your home screen
description: Add Avodah to your phone's home screen so it behaves like a native app.
---

Avodah is designed for the best experience on your phone, added to your home screen like a regular app. Steps are similar across devices — here's iPhone Safari as an example:

1. Open Avodah in Safari.
2. Tap the **Share** icon at the bottom of the screen.
3. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**.
4. Tap **Add** in the top-right corner.

Avodah will now appear as an icon on your home screen, and can be opened just like any other app.
