1. Open your phone's browser and go to the Avodah registration page.
2. Enter your name, email address, and a password.
3. Submit the form — you'll be logged in automatically once your account is created.
4. Continue to [installing Avodah on your home screen](/getting-started/installing-to-your-home-screen) for the best experience.

## Having trouble signing up?

If you don't receive a confirmation email within a few minutes, check your spam folder first. If it still hasn't arrived, contact your church administrator — they can look up your account and confirm it went through.
